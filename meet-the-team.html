The People Behind India Recycles

Passionate individuals driving a sustainable future for India's textile ecosystem.

Renu Pokharna

Co-Founder

Renu holds a degree in public policy from Columbia University, New York, served as a Chief Minister's Fellow in Gujarat and worked at a social enterprise in Uganda. She leads strategy, fundraising and external partnerships.

Pranav Gupta

Co-Founder

Pranav is a CSR and sustainability policy specialist who joined as co-founder to drive operational execution. He manages stakeholder partnerships, volunteer coordination and day-to-day programs across Ahmedabad and Vadodara.

India Recycles runs on the passion of 400+ volunteers who give their time every week.
