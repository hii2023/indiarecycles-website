How It Began

A Simple Observation,
A Big Change

India Recycles began in 2017 when co-founder Renu Pokharna organised a simple clothing swap event in Ahmedabad. Renu had studied public policy at Columbia University in New York, served as a Chief Minister's Fellow in Gujarat and worked at a social enterprise in Uganda. After returning to India, she was struck by how much the city's households discarded and how much its communities needed. The swap was a first answer.

Within months the small swap grew into something structured: a non-profit that collects donated clothes at drop points across the city, sorts them with the help of dedicated volunteers, and sells them at weekly community events in urban slum neighbourhoods. Every item is priced between ₹10 and ₹50 so that cost is never a barrier to dignity.

Every garment that cannot be resold finds a second life through our upcycling programs, Re-Vibe and the Godadi Project, creating handcrafted products and warm bedding made entirely from donated textiles and providing livelihoods to women in the communities we serve.

Today, India Recycles operates across Ahmedabad and Vadodara with 20+ drop locations, 400+ volunteers and over 35,000 lives touched through clothes alone. Surplus sales revenue funds medical camps, scholarships and livelihood programs.

What We Stand For

Our Purpose & Values

Our Mission

To create a circular economy for clothes in India by diverting textile waste from landfills, making quality clothing accessible to all, and generating livelihoods in the communities we serve.

Our Vision

A sustainable India where no textile goes to waste, every family has access to quality clothing regardless of income, and slum communities are economically empowered through dignified livelihoods.

Our Values

  • Dignity for every person
  • Zero waste commitment
  • Community empowerment
  • Transparency & trust
  • Environmental responsibility
The People

Our Team

Passionate individuals driving a sustainable future for India's textile ecosystem.

Renu Pokharna

Co-Founder

Renu holds a degree in public policy from Columbia University, New York, served as a Chief Minister's Fellow in Gujarat and worked at a social enterprise in Uganda.

Published in The Wall Street Journal, she returned to India in 2017 and founded India Recycles from a single clothing swap in Ahmedabad. She leads strategy, fundraising and external partnerships.

Pranav Gupta

Co-Founder

Pranav is a CSR and sustainability policy specialist who joined as co-founder to drive operational execution.

He manages stakeholder partnerships, volunteer coordination and day-to-day programs including community sales, medical camps and upcycling initiatives across Ahmedabad and Vadodara.
