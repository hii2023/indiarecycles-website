    How we estimate

    Each garment is given an average weight and its most common fabric. We then apply published recycling-impact factors for that fabric (carbon, water and energy saved per kilogram of textile diverted from landfill) and add everything up. These are careful estimates meant to show the scale of your contribution, not exact measurements.
