Global Goals

Environmental & Social Impact

Our work contributes directly to multiple UN Sustainable Development Goals and measurably reduces carbon footprint.

UN SDG Alignment

India Recycles directly addresses SDGs 1 (No Poverty), 8 (Decent Work), 10 (Reduced Inequalities), 12 (Responsible Consumption), and 13 (Climate Action).

Carbon Footprint Saved

Every recycled garment saves approximately 3–5 kg of CO₂ vs. producing new. 35,500+ clothes recycled means over 100 tonnes of CO₂ avoided from landfills.

Beyond Clothes

Community Programs

Revenue from our sales funds life-changing programs for the communities we serve.

Medical Camps

Regular health checkups, eye exams, and gynaecological services for slum residents. 1,000+ beneficiaries served so far.

Education Scholarships

School scholarships for underprivileged children from slum families. 25+ students supported to date.

Women's Livelihoods

REVIBE and Godadi stitching units provide dignified income for women from slum communities in Ahmedabad.
