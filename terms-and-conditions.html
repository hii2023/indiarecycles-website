About us

India Recycles is a non-profit organisation based in Gujarat, India. We collect donated clothing and give it a second life through community sales, thrift sales, upcycling and livelihood programmes.

Clothing donations

Items you drop at a collection point or hand to our team become the property of India Recycles. We decide how each item is best used: resale at a community or thrift sale, upcycling through Re-Vibe, or recycling. Items that cannot be used in any of those ways are handled through our zero waste process.

Please donate only clean, wearable items. We cannot accept anything spoiled, wet, torn beyond repair or otherwise unusable. We cannot return donated items once they are collected, and we cannot guarantee that a particular item will reach a particular person.

Financial donations

Financial contributions are voluntary and, as a rule, non-refundable, because funds are committed to programmes quickly. If you donated in error or were charged twice, email us within 7 days and we will look into a refund in good faith.

Payments are processed by regulated payment providers and your own bank. Tax benefits under Section 80G apply where eligible and where we have your details to issue a receipt. We are not able to give you tax advice.
