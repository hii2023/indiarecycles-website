Careers

Build a Second Life With Us

We are a small, hands-on team that cares deeply about people and the planet. If you want work that leaves communities better than you found them, we would love to hear from you.

Why Work With Us

Purpose first, people always.

Real impact

Your work translates directly into clothes, dignity and livelihoods for families across Gujarat.

Learn broadly

From operations to community work, a small team means you wear many hats and grow fast.

Good company

Work alongside volunteers, artisans and partners who show up with heart every single day.

Current Openings

We do not have formal openings listed right now, but we are always glad to meet people who share our mission. Send us a short note about yourself and how you would like to contribute.
