How It Works

Our Model

A circular economy for clothes, from your wardrobe to communities that need it most, with zero waste along the way.

How India Recycles Works

Every donation travels through these six stages, creating a complete circular economy for donated clothing.

STEP 1

You Donate

Drop your pre-loved clothes at any of our 20+ collection points across Ahmedabad and Vadodara. We accept all kinds of clothing.

STEP 2

Volunteers Sort

Our dedicated volunteers sort, clean and categorise all incoming donations, separating wearable items from those that need upcycling.

STEP 3

Community Sale

Wearable clothes go to our weekly sales in slum communities, priced between ₹10 and ₹50 so families buy with dignity, not charity.

STEP 4

Thrift Sale

Curated, higher-end pieces go to our Thrift Sale events for urban audiences, normalising pre-loved fashion in the city.

STEP 5

Re-Vibe Upcycling

Clothes that cannot be worn again are upcycled into Re-Vibe products: bags, accessories and home goods with a new life.

STEP 6

Zero Waste

Whatever is left becomes Godadi winter mattresses, stitched by women from the community. Zero textile waste, zero excuses.

Model 1

Community Sale

Every week we run clothing sales in slum communities across Ahmedabad and Vadodara, with items priced between ₹10 and ₹50. Charging a small price rather than giving clothes away lets families choose what they want and keeps their dignity intact.

Zero Waste

Where the money goes

Revenue Fuels Impact

Every rupee from our sales goes back into community programs, creating a self-sustaining model of social impact.

Health Programs

Medical camps, health checkups, eye exams and gynaecological services for slum residents who can't afford private healthcare.

Education Support

Scholarships for children from under-resourced families so they can stay in school. We've supported 25+ students so far.

Livelihood Creation

Re-Vibe and Godadi programs employ women from slum communities, turning waste into income and empowerment.
