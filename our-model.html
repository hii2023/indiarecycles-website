How It Works

Our Model

A circular economy for clothes, from your wardrobe to communities that need it most, with zero waste along the way.

How India Recycles Works

Four simple steps that create a complete circular economy for donated clothing.

STEP 1

You Donate

Drop your pre-loved clothes at any of our 20+ collection points across Ahmedabad and Vadodara. We accept all kinds of clothing.

STEP 2

Volunteers Sort

Our dedicated volunteers sort, clean and categorise all incoming donations, separating wearable items from those that need upcycling.

STEP 3

Slum & Yearly Sales

Good clothes go to weekly slum sales (₹10–₹50) or our biannual Yearly Sale events for urban audiences.

STEP 4

Zero Waste Upcycling

Remaining clothes become REVIBE upcycled products or Godadi winter mattresses. Zero textile waste, zero excuses.

Model 1

Slum Sales

Every week, India Recycles organises clothing sales in urban slum communities across Ahmedabad and Vadodara. Items are priced between ₹10 and ₹50, making quality clothing accessible to families who need it most.

By charging a small price rather than giving clothes for free, we help our customers gain a sense of responsibility and maintain their dignity. The sales are organised events, not handouts.

Model 2

Yearly Sale

Twice a year, India Recycles organises curated second-hand clothing events targeting urban, affluent audiences in Ahmedabad. These events showcase higher-end donated items at affordable prices.

The goal is to normalise the concept of using pre-loved items in urban, moneyed populations, shifting cultural attitudes towards sustainable consumption.

Where the money goes

Revenue Fuels Impact

Every rupee from our sales goes back into community programs, creating a self-sustaining model of social impact.

Health Programs

Medical camps, health checkups, eye exams and gynaecological services for slum residents who can't afford private healthcare.

Education Support

Scholarships for underprivileged children so they can stay in school. We've supported 25+ students so far.

Livelihood Creation

REVIBE and Godadi programs employ women from slum communities, turning waste into income and empowerment.
