ReVibe Upcycling

Old Cloth, New Life

ReVibe is our upcycling line. Garments that cannot be resold are reborn through the hands of women artisans into quilts, bags and home goods. Nothing is wasted, and every stitch creates a dignified livelihood.

Shop ReVibe Support the Program

How ReVibe Works

A simple loop that turns worn textiles into something worth keeping.

01

Collect and sort

Donated clothes that are too worn to resell are set aside for upcycling instead of going to landfill.

02

Transform by hand

Women artisans cut, stitch and quilt the fabric into new products, earning a fair wage for their craft.

03

Reimagine and shop

Finished pieces find new homes through our thrift store, closing the loop on textile waste.
