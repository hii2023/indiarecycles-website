Request a Pickup

Tell us where you are and roughly how many clothes you have. Our team will get in touch to arrange a collection.

We usually respond within 2 to 3 working days. Pickups depend on volunteer availability in your area.

Want to Host a Drop Point?

If you have a workspace, housing society, or office that can serve as a collection point, we'd love to partner with you. Setup is free. We manage everything.

What Can You Donate?

We accept a wide variety of clothing, in any condition.

Any Clothing

Shirts, pants, sarees, kurtas, kids' wear and more, all accepted.

All Conditions

Wearable, lightly worn or heavily worn, we'll find a use for every piece.

Clean & Bagged

Please wash and bag your donations before dropping them off.
