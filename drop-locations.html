What Can You Donate?

We accept a wide variety of clothing, in any condition.

Any Clothing

Shirts, pants, sarees, kurtas, kids' wear and more, all accepted.

All Conditions

Wearable, lightly worn or heavily worn, we'll find a use for every piece.

Clean & Bagged

Please wash and bag your donations before dropping them off.

India Recycles

Giving donated clothes a second life through community slum sales, upcycling and livelihood creation across Ahmedabad & Vadodara.
