Our Partners

Our Network

Together We Create Impact

From government bodies to design studios, our partner network spans four key categories, all united by a commitment to sustainability.

Category 01

Government, NGOs &
Educational Institutions

We collaborate with government bodies, non-profit organisations and academic institutions that share our mission of building a more equitable and sustainable society. These partners help us expand our reach, co-run health camps, and support educational scholarship programs for children from under-resourced families.

  • Co-run community health camps
  • Student scholarship support
  • Awareness & policy advocacy
Category 02

Corporate Partners

Offices and corporations across Ahmedabad and Vadodara host our collection boxes, making it effortless for thousands of employees to donate clothes they no longer wear. These partnerships form the backbone of our donation network and are fully CSR-eligible with documentation provided.

  • Drop box installed & managed by us
  • Monthly impact reports
  • CSR certificates provided
Category 03

Hospitality Partners

Hotels, clubs, gyms and recreational spaces host our drop boxes in high-footfall areas, making it convenient for members and guests to donate. These partnerships help us reach a wider donor base and keep our collection points accessible and visible across both cities.

  • High-footfall collection points
  • Guest & member engagement
  • Zero effort from property side
Category 04

Design Partners

Our design partners collaborate on the creative side of our ReVibe upcycled product line, bringing craft skill, design sensibility and market access to help us create beautiful products from donated textiles. Together we prove that sustainable can also be stylish and commercially viable.

  • ReVibe product collaboration
  • Craft & design expertise
  • Market & retail access
