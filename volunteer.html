Volunteer

Give a Few Hours, Change a Life

Volunteers are the heart of India Recycles. Whether you have an afternoon or a weekend, there is a way for you to help clothes find their second home.

Ways to Help

Pick what fits your time and your strengths.

Sorting and packing

Help us receive, sort and pack donated clothes so they reach the right communities in good shape.

Slum sale days

Join our weekly community sales, set up stalls and spend the day with the families we serve.

Skills and outreach

Lend your skills in design, photography, social media or fundraising to widen our reach.

Host a drive

Run a clothing collection drive at your office, society or school and rally your circle.

Sign Up to Volunteer

Tell us a little about yourself and how you would like to help. We will get back to you with the next steps.
