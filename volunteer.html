Give a Few Hours, Change a Life

Volunteers are the heart of India Recycles. Whether you have an afternoon or a weekend, there is a way for you to help clothes find their second home.

Ways to Help

Pick what fits your time and your strengths.

Sorting and packing

Receive, sort and pack donated clothes so they reach the right communities in good shape.

Community sale days

Join our weekly community sales, set up stalls and spend the day with the families we serve.

Skills and outreach

Lend design, photography, social media or fundraising skills to widen our reach.
