Every impact number on the website, in one place. Set a monthly average and the numbers rise on their own.

From the Counting since month onward, the website adds that monthly amount automatically over time, so the figures keep climbing without you editing anything. Leave "Avg added / month" at 0 to keep a number fixed.

Website Images

Replace any image on the website - no code needed. Pictures are grouped by page and by the section they appear in, and the line under each one tells you exactly where it shows up.

How it works: Click Replace under a picture, choose a new one, then click Save Changes to publish. It stays live until you replace it again or click Reset.
Why a photo can look repeated: some sections show one photo on computers and the same photo cropped differently on phones. Those now share a single card marked Web + Mobile, so you upload once and both views change together.
