Our Mission

Giving clothes a
second life

India Recycles collects donated clothes from households and redistributes them through weekly sales in urban slum communities, priced between ₹10 and ₹50 to preserve dignity and promote responsibility.

Clothes that can no longer be worn are upcycled into shoes and apparel through our Re-Vibe brand, or transformed into traditional winter mattresses through the Godadi Project, creating livelihoods for women.

How It Works

Our Model

A full circle - from your wardrobe to communities that need it most.

1

You Donate

Drop clothes at any of our 20+ collection points across the city.

2

We Sort

Volunteers sort, clean and prepare each item for its next life.

3

Community Sale

Weekly sales in slum communities. Clothes from ₹10 to ₹50.

4

Thrift Sale

Curated pre-loved pieces at events for urban audiences.

5

Re-Vibe

Unwearable clothes are upcycled into bags, accessories and home goods.

6

Zero Waste

Whatever is left becomes Godadi winter mattresses. Nothing is wasted.
