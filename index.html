Our Mission

Giving clothes a
second life

India Recycles collects donated clothes from households and redistributes them through weekly sales in urban slum communities, priced between ₹10 and ₹50 to preserve dignity and promote responsibility.

Clothes that can no longer be worn are upcycled into shoes and apparel through our REVIBE brand, or transformed into traditional winter mattresses through the Godadi Project, creating livelihoods for women in slum communities.
